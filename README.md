A one-star review of Monday, written as if Monday were a restaurant. 25 words or fewer, one line, plain text.

> One star. The waiter took my order, nodded, and brought me five days of work. I asked for the manager. He was me.

`main.ts` (node22) writes that line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. No dependencies, no network, no build step.
